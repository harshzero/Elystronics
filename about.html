The Heritage of Elystronics Repair Shop

Elystronics Repair Shop was established in Poblacion V, Sta. Cruz, Laguna, born out of a commitment to provide accessible, dependable, and technically sound repair solutions for Filipino families.

In today's throwaway culture where broken appliances are quickly discarded, shop technicians Raul Z. Esperanza and Arman firmly believe: "If a beloved appliance can be restored properly, safely, and durably, why spend tens of thousands of pesos buying new?"

With over two decades of practical experience spanning authorized electronic service centers and field diagnostics, Elystronics implements strict technical protocols. From electrostatic discharge (ESD) safe component soldering to digital oscilloscope signal tracing, every repair is conducted with engineering precision.

"Our reputation is built on straightforward honesty. We never invent problems. If a simple failed capacitor or blown fuse is the issue, that is all you will ever be charged for."

— Raul Z. Esperanza, Electronic Technician

Standard Operating Procedure

Our Quality and Safety Standards

Every piece of electronics entering Elystronics Repair Shop undergoes systematic diagnostics.

01

Visual & Safety Inspection

Initial check for cracked enclosures, scorched traces, rodent wire damage, and ground insulation isolation prior to applying line power.

02

Circuit & Mechanical Diagnostics

Precise voltage rail measurements with calibrated multimeters: 3.3V standby, 5V, 12V, 24V rails, freon manifold pressure, and motor winding resistance.

03

Precision Component Replacement

Professional micro-soldering with lead-free solder and high-temperature rated components (e.g. 105°C low-ESR capacitors for longevity).

04

Continuous Load Burn-In Test

Appliance is operated for 2 to 4 hours under regular operating loads to ensure thermal stability before handing over to the customer.
